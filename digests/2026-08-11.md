### [Announcing: Microsoft Fabric Item Recovery will be enabled by default for tenants without an explicit setting](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/updates?id=569140)

**Status:** Effective | **Published:** 2026-08-10 | **Products:** Microsoft Fabric | **Tags:** Announcement

Starting August 23, 2026, Microsoft Fabric will enable Item Recovery by default for tenants that have not explicitly configured the setting.

- Supported item types will have a 3-day recovery window by default.
- Admins can review or change this setting in the Fabric admin portal under Tenant settings > Item Recovery.
