## Networking

### [Generally Available: 2.2X IDPS performance optimization in Azure Firewall](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/updates?id=569256)

**Status:** Launched | **Published:** 2026-08-11 | **Products:** Azure Firewall | **Tags:** Features

Azure Firewall Premium now offers a 120% increase in IDPS throughput performance, significantly enhancing security inspection capabilities for enterprise environments.

- Throughput with TLS inspection and IDPS in Deny mode increased from 10 Gbps to 22 Gbps.
- Per TCP connection throughput with IDPS enabled doubled from 300 Mbps to 600 Mbps.
- Performance boost is enabled by default and includes Accelerated Networking on firewall VMs.
- Improved throughput supports highly sensitive and regulated environments requiring advanced firewall capabilities.

Documentation: [Azure Firewall performance](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/firewall/firewall-performance)
